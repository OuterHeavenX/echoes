# RELEASE NOTES — v0.2.0-slice

Echoes of the Last Dawn, vertical slice (Prologue + Chapter 1).

## What you can play
Bellweather during Lanternwake, the disappearance, the station
undercroft, and the Extraction Warden boss. Three heroes (Rowan, Mira,
Garran), ATB battles with Wait/Active modes, resonance team techniques,
a shop and inn, equipment and JP job unlocks, two side quests, and a
chapter-end transition. Saves: 3 slots + autosave, plus JSON export/
import for moving saves between browser and Linux.

## What is not in this build
Chapters 2–8, the epilogue and postgame, five more heroes, nine more
jobs, the overworld, and most side content. See docs/CONTENT_MANIFEST.md
for the exact implemented-vs-planned count. This slice is a playable
foundation, not the finished 20+ hour campaign.

## Known issues
- Device testing (iPhone, iPad, Android, Steam Deck hardware) is pending.
- Battle-speed menu control, font/UI scaling, and key remapping are not
  built yet.
- Music is simple generated loops, not final composed tracks.
- Browser save persistence in private browsing / restricted storage
  may fail; use the JSON export as a backup.
